KYC POLICY - TEST NBFC

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Compliance

1. INTRODUCTION

This Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy outlines our commitment to preventing money laundering and terrorist financing activities.

2. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION

2.1 Individual Customers
All individual customers must provide:
- Valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Driving License)
- Proof of address not older than 3 months
- Recent passport-size photograph

2.2 Corporate Customers
Corporate entities must submit:
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Board Resolution authorizing account opening
- PAN card of the company
- Identity proof of beneficial owners holding 25% or more stake

3. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)

3.1 Risk Classification
Customers shall be classified into Low, Medium, and High risk categories based on:
- Nature of business
- Geographic location
- Transaction patterns
- Ownership structure

3.2 Enhanced Due Diligence
High-risk customers require:
- Senior management approval
- Additional verification of source of funds
- Enhanced ongoing monitoring

4. ONGOING MONITORING

4.1 Transaction Monitoring
All customer transactions shall be monitored for unusual patterns.

4.2 Periodic Review
Customer information shall be updated:
- Low risk: Every 3 years
- Medium risk: Every 2 years
- High risk: Annually

5. RECORD KEEPING

5.1 All KYC records shall be maintained for a minimum period of 10 years from the date of cessation of relationship.

5.2 Records must be readily retrievable for regulatory inspections.

6. COMPLIANCE OFFICER

6.1 A Principal Officer shall be designated to ensure compliance with this policy.

6.2 The Principal Officer shall report suspicious transactions to FIU-IND within prescribed timelines.

7. TRAINING

7.1 All staff handling customer onboarding shall receive KYC training annually.

7.2 Training records shall be maintained for audit purposes.

8. POLICY REVIEW

This policy shall be reviewed annually or whenever there are regulatory changes.
